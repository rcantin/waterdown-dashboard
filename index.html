How to read this dashboard

  • Year-end totals come from Hamilton Police Service ward reports compiled for 2025.
  • The official live map covers only the past 60 days and five offence types, posted with a one-day delay and block-level offset.
  • Incident pins here are compiled from public HPS releases and local reporting. They are not a complete occurrence list.
  • Division 3 (Waterdown, Flamborough, Dundas, Ancaster and nearby) had the highest youth-crime volume city-wide in 2025.
