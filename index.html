Community Projects & Construction

  • Ongoing

    Grindstone Creek Bridge replacement

    Dundas St E between Mill and Reynold. Stage 1 underway since fall 2025: south half removed, foundations in progress; one-lane alternating traffic (eastbound 2 a.m.–2 p.m., westbound 2 p.m.–2 a.m.). Full closures used for girder lifts. Target completion fall 2026.

    City project updates →
  • Ongoing

    Waterdown Road widening

    Craven Ave (Aldershot) to Mountain Brow Rd. Reconstruction and widening with multi-use path, curbs, storm sewers, and watermain work (Hamilton/Burlington). Started 2024; lane reductions ongoing. Substantial completion expected by end of 2026.

    Burlington project page →
  • Ongoing

    Combined police & fire station

    Hwy 6 & Parkside Dr. Ground broken summer 2025 on the ~$55M facility with 9-1-1 dispatch and collision reporting centre. About 40 officers and full-time fire staffing planned. Target opening spring 2027.

    Coverage & details →
  • Planned

    Clappison’s Corners (Hwy 5 & 6) interchange

    MTO partial cloverleaf (Parclo) interchange replacing the signalized intersection, plus multi-use paths and commuter parking. Design/EA complete; construction expected to start in 2026 and run several years (into ~2028–29). Property expropriations underway.

Crime Data · Ward 15

Ward 15 totals — 2025

12th of 15 wards for break-ins. Property crime above the ward’s 5-year average (~433).

Year over year (property crime)

2024 ≈ 513 · 2025 ≈ 522 (+~2%). Vehicle thefts in Waterdown trended down into mid-2025.

Nearby wards — property crime & break-ins (2025)

Comparable suburban / west-end wards. Ward 15 sits near Dundas and Glanbrook on volume; Ancaster is higher.

Ward 15 highlights

  • • 2025: 522 property crimes, 47 break & enters, 79 auto thefts, 57 thefts from auto (HPS / compiled ward rankings).
  • • Property crime roughly flat vs 2024 (~513) but above the 5-year average (~433).
  • • Waterdown vehicle thefts fell (85 in 2024 → 56 reported by mid-2025).
  • • Division 3 (includes Waterdown/Flamborough) had the highest youth crime volume city-wide in 2025.

Community Schools

  • HWDSB · Public elementary

    Allan A. Greenleaf Elementary

    JK–8 · Parkside Dr, Waterdown. Neighbourhood school serving central Waterdown; part of the boundary review for the new Mountainview Heights school.

    Mary Hopkins Elementary

    JK–4 English & Grades 1–4 French Immersion · Mill St N, Waterdown. Smaller primary-focused school; FI continues into intermediate grades at Guy B. Brown.

    Guy B. Brown Elementary

    JK–8 English & French Immersion · Braeheid Ave, Waterdown. Hosts intermediate FI for the Waterdown family of schools.

    Flamborough Centre Elementary

    JK–8 · Centre Rd. Rural/suburban school serving areas north of Waterdown village; English track after recent FI program changes.

    Balaclava Elementary

    JK–8 · 10th Concession E (Carlisle / Freelton area). Serves northern Flamborough communities.

    Millgrove Elementary

    JK–8 · Millgrove. Small rural school with a close-knit community feel.

  • HWDSB · Public secondary (local)

    Waterdown District High School

    Grades 9–12 · Parkside Dr. Home secondary school for Waterdown and Flamborough; offers a range of academic and specialist pathways.

  • HWDSB · Common secondary pathways (outside ward)

    Westdale Secondary School

    Grades 9–12 · Westdale. French Immersion secondary pathway many Ward 15 FI students continue into after elementary FI at Guy B. Brown / Mary Hopkins.

    Glendale Secondary School

    Grades 9–12 · East Hamilton. Audition-based arts program; used by Ward 15 students seeking specialized arts pathways.

    Westmount Secondary School

    Grades 9–12 · West Mountain. Self-directed / alternative learning model often chosen by Ward 15 families looking for a more flexible secondary experience.

  • HWCDSB · Catholic elementary

    Guardian Angels Catholic Elementary

    JK–8 · Centre Rd, Waterdown. Long-standing Catholic school beside St. Thomas the Apostle Parish; recent classroom and daycare addition.

    St. Thomas the Apostle Catholic Elementary

    JK–8 · Skinner Rd, Waterdown. Newer school (opened 2013) serving east Waterdown growth areas.

    Our Lady of Mount Carmel Catholic Elementary

    JK–8 · Carlisle. Serves Carlisle and surrounding rural Flamborough Catholic families.

  • HWCDSB · Catholic secondary

    St. Mary Catholic Secondary School

    Grades 9–12 · West Hamilton. Common secondary destination for Catholic elementary students from Guardian Angels, St. Thomas the Apostle, and Our Lady of Mount Carmel.

  • Coming soon

    New Waterdown Elementary (Mountainview Heights)

    JK–8 · Skinner Rd & Great Falls Blvd. HWDSB school under construction for the 2027–28 school year: ~504 student spaces plus an 88-space child care centre. Boundary review underway with Allan A. Greenleaf, Mary Hopkins, Guy B. Brown, Flamborough Centre and Balaclava.
